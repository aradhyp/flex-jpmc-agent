# FLEX Product Brief

## Product name

**FLEX — Financial Liberator and Enablement Xcaliber**

## Vision

FLEX helps 16–19 year-old young earners move from first income to financial sustainability. It combines youth-friendly coaching, income planning, age-aware product recommendations, and guardrails around credit.

## Problem

Young people who begin earning in high school or just before college often face four gaps:

1. They receive income but do not know how to split it.
2. They do not know which banking product fits their age and independence level.
3. They can mistake credit access for financial freedom.
4. They need fast, minimal guidance that does not feel like a traditional bank form.

## Target users

- 16–17 year-old high-school students with part-time, freelance, creator, or family-business income.
- 18–19 year-old college-bound or early-college earners who need checking, savings, and credit education.
- Parents/guardians who need a safe enablement flow for minors.

## Core value proposition

FLEX gives young users a simple sequence:

**Earn → Save first → Spend safely → Avoid fees → Learn credit → Build independence**

## MVP features

- Profile onboarding: age, stage, income pattern, goal, independence level.
- Money map: future goal, emergency buffer, needs, and flexible spend.
- Product pathway: age-aware Chase product recommendations.
- Credit readiness gate: credit education before card recommendation.
- Ask FLEX: simple guided coaching layer.
- Compliance-first UX: no SSN, no login credentials, no account numbers.

## Chase product mapping used in the prototype

| User scenario | FLEX product suggestion |
|---|---|
| Age 16–17, parent/guardian involved | Chase High School Checking℠ |
| Age 17–19, ready for simple independent checking | Chase Secure Banking℠ |
| Any young earner with a savings goal | Chase Savings℠ + Autosave |
| Age 18–19, wants to understand credit | Chase Credit Journey® |
| Age 18–19, credit-ready | Chase Freedom Rise® |

## Guardrail philosophy

FLEX does not maximize product conversion at the expense of safety. It recommends the safest next step for the user's age and readiness.

- Minors are routed to guardian-supported banking education.
- Credit is hidden for users under 18.
- Credit card recommendations require readiness checks.
- Product recommendations explain “why this fits you.”
- FLEX does not collect sensitive financial identifiers.

## Interview positioning

This prototype demonstrates product thinking, customer segmentation, UX simplification, compliance sensitivity, and AI-agent orchestration.
